# Album 82- Sword Over the Empires - Terracotta Legions and the Golden Blade
Song 1 of Album 82 confronts empire-anomalies from 2101–2200: terracotta tomb-legions, Sumerian–Akkadian sorcery, the masked mind-enslaving king, Egypt’s golden blade, Persian and Achaemenid ambition, Indian/Harappan idols, desert oracles, false scribes, trade spirits, bot systems, and linked national designations (FR-240, CN-2100, JP-2101/2102/2199). The prayer interlinks ancient Near Eastern, Egyptian, Persian, Indian, and polar echoes, then ends in Christ’s victory by the Sword of the Word of God.
