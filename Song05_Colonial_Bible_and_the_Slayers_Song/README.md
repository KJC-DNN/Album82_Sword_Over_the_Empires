# Album 82 – Sword Over the Empires
## Colonial Bible and the Slayer’s Song
Song 5 completes the colonial thread of Song 4 and covers 2501–2600: India, ledgers, French–British and American colonial ambition, Ethiopia, Jamaica, Ghana and other African nations, Colonial Bible, Sumerian tyranny-threads, CN/JP designations, and bot systems. Official wiki items are named for banishing: SCP-2301 (Slayer’s Song / oni / dragon), SCP-2401 (hive-colony), SCP-2501 (The Claw), SCP-2522 (hatbot), SCP-2548, SCP-2571, SCP-2600. All are dismantled by the Sword of Christ.
